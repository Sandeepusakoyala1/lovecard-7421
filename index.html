My love,

I’m so sorry. I’ve been sitting with it, and I know my words and my actions hurt you. That was never what I wanted, and you deserved better from me.

You are the one person who makes my days softer. When I let you down, I let down the best part of my life, and it breaks my heart to know I caused you pain.

I’m not asking you to forget it. I’m asking for the chance to listen better, to be more patient, and to show you with my actions, not only my words, how much you mean to me.

Thank you for every time you’ve forgiven me, held my hand, and loved me when I wasn’t easy to love. I promise to keep earning that.

Always yours, with all my heart.

This little bear is sorry too.

He’s holding my heart for you.
